Character creation

Your slayer starts with a roll.

Take your chances with six attributes: Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Constitution, and Charisma. Your rolls bring bonuses, penalties, and a different starting point for the journey ahead.

Prefer an even footing? Choose a premade character with no attribute bonuses or penalties.

Know your attributes. Scores of 9–12 are average. Higher scores bring bonuses; lower scores bring penalties.

Weapons, armor & supplies

Prepare for what waits in the dark.

Gather gold and visit the shop for weapons, armor, rings, amulets, and potions. Every purchase is another way to prepare for a hostile world.

Equip new items from your inventory, then sell what you no longer need to fund your next upgrade.

  • Weapons & armor
  • Rings & amulets

  • Equipment to buy & sell

The hunt

Answer the Order’s call.

Werewolves, trolls, vampires, and other creatures threaten the region. Visit the questboard to learn where the Order needs help, then set out to earn gold and items for your efforts.

Face creatures in one-on-one combat. Keep health potions ready, watch your condition, and retreat when an encounter becomes too dangerous.

Live to hunt again. Some creatures are far more dangerous than others. Knowing when to escape is part of surviving.

A place among the Order

Return stronger.

Back at the Order, visit the healer to recover from battle, collect supplies, and choose your next task. Each creature defeated moves your adventurer toward a higher level.

Take your progress to Game Center: earn achievements, compare high scores, and challenge friends to see who can advance their character the furthest.

  • Healing between battles
  • Gold & quest rewards
  • Character progression
  • Game Center challenges
