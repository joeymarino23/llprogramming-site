Warden of the Wilds

Guard the hearth. Know the wild. Keep the balance.

Beyond your walls, the wilds hold secrets, danger, and the means to build a home worth defending. Become a Warden in a fantasy RPG where every expedition leads back to your stronghold.

Beyond the walls

Learn the land.
Uncover what lies beneath.

Begin in the Whispering Forest, where winding paths lead to hidden shrines, goblin territory, and creatures changed by a spreading blight. Explore, hunt, and bring your discoveries home.

A world to understand

Find hidden locations and face choices shaped by your attributes. Record the creatures you encounter in your Bestiary, learning their intentions, drops, and weaknesses.

A reason to return

Bring back gold, materials, equipment, and mysterious scrolls. Secure your expedition’s rewards at home, recover, and prepare for the next journey.

Your Warden, your calling

Strength. Spellcraft. Faith.

Choose a Warrior, Mage, or Cleric, shape your attributes, and develop the abilities that define your approach to the wilds.

01 / WARRIOR

Master the blade

Build around martial techniques, physical strength, and well-timed defenses. Meet the dangers of the frontier head-on.

02 / MAGE

Study the arcane

Manage Mana, uncover scrolls, and learn permanent spells. Expand your Spellbook and prepare your favorites for battle.

03 / CLERIC

Answer the sacred

Draw on Divine Favor for sacred abilities and healing. Balance protection and offense as you face the threats beyond your walls.

Choose where your chronicle begins. Claim the Abandoned Keep, Frontier Hamlet, or Mountain Outpost—three stronghold origins with their own advantages.

Read the threat. Choose your moment.

Every action has its time.

Combat runs on Initiative. Bank it, spend it, and decide when to attack, defend, or use an ability. Visible enemy intentions help you plan your next move.

  • Prepare for the encounterEquip your Warden and choose the techniques or spells you want close at hand.
  • Learn your enemiesUse observed intentions and discovered weaknesses to guide your decisions.
  • Bring the lessons homeTurn hard-won loot and experience into a stronger Warden and a better-prepared stronghold.

A home worth defending

Build more than walls.

Raise buildings, manage supplies, recruit soldiers, and prepare your defenses. When raiders come, your choices at home matter as much as your strength in the field.

Grow your stronghold

Invest expedition rewards in construction and recruitment. Keep an eye on supplies, wages, and morale as your settlement grows.

Stand with your people

Prepare for incoming raids and issue tactical orders through the defense. At home or away, your preparations shape the outcome.

The chronicle is taking shape

The wilds will be waiting.
