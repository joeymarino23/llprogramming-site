Tactical spell combat

The enemy tells you what comes next. What you do with that knowledge is yours.

Build around cooldowns, status effects, elemental affinities, and reactions. Save a spell for the next phase, spend ingredients to cast it now, or trust your at-will attack while the rest of your spellbook recovers.

  • Six-slot spell loadouts
  • Visible enemy intent
  • Elemental reactions
  • Weaknesses and resistances
  • Status-driven tactics
  • Multi-phase region bosses

A story that stays with you

Follow the notes your mentor left behind.

Trace Master Elowen through skippable region introductions, checkpoint discoveries, boss revelations, and region endings. Every recovered page is preserved in the Field Journal, so the story remains available without interrupting Replay Hunts.
