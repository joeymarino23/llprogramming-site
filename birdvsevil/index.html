An uneasy alliance

The heroes are gone.
The exile has returned.

Once banished for practicing dark magic, the necromancer Sabbath returns to a kingdom overrun by the Lord of Darkness. At the abandoned Dark Base, he summons the Dark Bird of the Abyss.

You are his apprentice, helping control the creature that could free Trismore. But with dark magic at your command, saving the kingdom may come at a cost.

Follow the kingdom's unusual quests and seek out the mysterious Mox as your journey unfolds.
